Press Release
Change in indices w.e.f. February 3, 1999
The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee  of India Index Services & Products Ltd. (IISL)
met on December 28, 1998 to finalise changes in various indices.
The changes being made in the various indices is enclosed:
(1) CNX Nifty Junior
The companies being excluded from CNX Nifty Junior are:
S. No. Company Name Avg. Impact Cost (%) Current Market
Capitalisation(Rs. mill.)
1 Sundaram Fastners Ltd. 1.89 3,240
2 Sundaram Finance Ltd. 1.80 3,360
The companies being included in CNX Nifty Junior are:
S. No. Company Name Avg. Impact Cost (%) Current Market
Capitalisation(Rs. mill.)
1 Corporation Bank Ltd. 0.36 9,924
2 Novartis (India) Ltd. 0.94 25,933
Sundaram Fastners Ltd. & Sundaram Finance Ltd. have been excluded since their market
impact cost has increased (securities are not trading at an average impact cost of less than
2.5% for 85% of the times) and market capitalisation has fallen.
With these changes the total market capitalisation of CNX Nifty Junior will increase from Rs.
3,48,649 millions to Rs. 3,77,346 millions. The total market capitalisation of the new CNX
Nifty Junior accounts for 9.36% of the total market capitalisation on the NSE. 
A list comprising the constituents of the old and the new CNX Nifty Junior alongwith their
weightages and market capitalisation is enclosed as Annexure I
.
