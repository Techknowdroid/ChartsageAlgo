Press Release
Change in S&P CNX 500 index w.e.f. March 17, 1999
The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee  of India Index Services & Products Ltd. (IISL)
met on February 25, 1999 to finalise changes in S&P CNX 500 Equity Index on account of
some companies being withdrawn by National Stock Exchange of India from its permitted to
trade category from March 17, 1999. From the companies withdrawn two of them are
constituents of S&P CNX 500 Equity Index.
The changes being made in S&P CNX 500 Equity Index is enclosed:
The companies being excluded from S&P CNX 500 are:
S. No. Company Name Industry Name
1 Precision Fasteners Limited Fasteners
2 Viral Filaments Limited Textile - Synthetics
The companies being included in the S&P CNX 500 are:
S. No. Company Name Industry Name
1 Moser Baer Ltd. Computers - Hardware
2 Aurobindo Pharma Ltd. Pharmaceuticals
The above changes will come into effect on March 17, 1999.
Place: Mumbai
Date : February 25, 1999
